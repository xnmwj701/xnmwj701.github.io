  <div class="section-heading"><h2 id="join-title">Prospective Students / Join Us</h2></div>
  <p class="join-audience">
    For prospective M.S. and Ph.D. students, research assistants and interns, and motivated
    undergraduates who would like to become involved with the lab early.
  </p>
  <p>
    Students with strong research curiosity and initiative are welcome to contact
    <a href="mailto:{{ site.author.email }}">{{ site.author.email }}</a>. Please include a CV
    and a brief introduction outlining your background and research interests.
  </p>
  <aside class="light-callout">
    Priority will be given to applicants with publications in leading international journals or
    conferences, awards in high-level robotics or programming competitions, or substantial
    experience in industry research projects.
  </aside>

  <div class="join-stack">
    <div class="stacked-info-block">
      <h3>Training Model</h3>
      <p>
        Students receive guidance throughout the research process, from problem formulation and
        theoretical development to experimental design, paper writing, and revision. Day-to-day
        management is flexible, while academic standards remain rigorous and emphasize independent
        thinking and creativity.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="stacked-info-block">
      <h3>Basic Requirements</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>A background in robot learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, VLA systems, world models, or robotic hardware platforms; alternatively, a strong foundation in applied mathematics or nonlinear control.</li>
        <li>Familiarity with current AIGC algorithms or open-source tools, together with proficiency in Python, PyTorch, MATLAB, C, or C++.</li>
        <li>The ability to conduct research toward high-quality publications or contribute to industry-scale research projects.</li>
        <li>Integrity, rigor, effective collaboration, and a firm commitment to academic ethics.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
